# Code of Conduct

Hearth is built around a warm hearth, not a cold trading floor. We hold
contributors to a simple standard:

- **Be kind and respectful.** Assume good faith. Critique ideas, not people.
- **Be welcoming.** Newcomers and non-experts are the whole point of this project.
- **No harassment**, discrimination, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, chat,
  or anywhere the community gathers.
- **Keep it honest.** No hype, no scams, no misleading claims about the network.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may ban, temporarily or
permanently, anyone whose behavior they judge harmful to the community.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers via a GitHub Security
Advisory or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This is a condensed adaptation of the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

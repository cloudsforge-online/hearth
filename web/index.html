The problem

Crypto broke the promise. Hearth keeps it.

Bitcoin became a farm. Everything else became a casino. Hearth fixes the specific things that pushed ordinary people out of their own money.

🏭 → 🏠 No farms

A RandomX-class, memory-hard puzzle plus a non-outsourceable signature means a warehouse of ASICs earns no more per dollar than your laptop — and pools can't form.

🪙 Spends like cash

Disinflationary emission into a perpetual tail, offset by a fee burn. Net inflation trends toward zero, so EMBER is stable money — not a hoarded collectible.

⚡ Fast & cheap

15-second blocks, tiny predictable fees, and instant retail payments over Tab channels. Buy a coffee without waiting or overpaying.

🔒 Cash-like privacy

Stealth addresses by default. Your balance is yours; your rent isn't the cashier's business.

🛡️ Never a fee cliff

The tail emission funds security forever, so Hearth doesn't inherit Bitcoin's "what happens when rewards end?" problem.

🌱 Funded by the Commons

10% of every block funds an on-chain treasury governed by the community. Development without VCs owning your money.

Anyone can do it

Mining is one click.

Install the Hearth app and press Start your hearth. It quietly uses spare CPU cycles — polite mining that respects your battery, heat, and whatever else you're doing. The longer your fire burns, the more EMBER lands in your wallet.

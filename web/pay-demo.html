Mockup · Hearth Pay

What accepting EMBER should look like.

This page is a mockup, not a product. The button on the right simulates its own settlement after 1.2 seconds and reports an invented transaction id. Nothing is charged, nothing is broadcast, and no node is consulted. Hearth Pay does not exist yet — do not put this on a shop.

The shape we are aiming at: no merchant account, no custodian, no chargebacks, no 3% card fee. The customer's wallet pays, and your page gets a callback once the transaction is buried deep enough to act on. As a sketch, that is:

<script src="hearth-pay-demo.js"></script> <div data-hearth-pay-demo data-to="ember1qmerchant7a3f…" data-amount="12.50" data-label="Corner Bakery"></div> <script> window.addEventListener('hearth:demo-paid', e => { console.log('SIMULATED', e.detail.txid, e.detail.demo); // demo === true }); </script>

What is real today: the node already reports what a merchant would need to check a payment — per-UTXO height and spendability from GET /address/:addr, and confirmation depth from GET /tx/:txid. What is missing is the wallet handoff: opening a hearth: URI and learning whether anything happened. That is the work, and it has not been done.
